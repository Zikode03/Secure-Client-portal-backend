# CSD in Phase 4

CSD is tracked as a standing National Treasury supplier-registration requirement, not as a tax return.

The compliance profile records whether the client supplies government, whether CSD registration is confirmed, and the CSD supplier number. When government-supplier applicability is confirmed, automation creates a CSD obligation and checks for `csd_registration_report` evidence.

No statutory filing due day is invented for CSD. The obligation uses the normal evidence, preparation and review workflow, and does not require an external filing submission or payment step.
